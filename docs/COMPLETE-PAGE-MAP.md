## Count interpretation

24 `<Route>` elements in source: 19 primary declarations (including `/demo` and `/room/my` redirects), 4 compatibility redirects, wildcard. There are 20 source files in `src/pages`, including unreferenced `MyDashboard.tsx`. `/join` is a single route with two conditional page implementations. A precise unique “page” count depends on whether conditional views and not-found are counted; the source facts above avoid conflating files, route entries, and rendered experiences.
